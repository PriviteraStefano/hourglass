# Hourglass — For Employees

Hourglass records what actually happened at work — once, at the source — so you never re-type it into a spreadsheet and never chase an approval. This page walks through your daily loop, what each status means, and what happens when an entry is rejected.

## The daily loop

1. **Capture** your work — hours against an activity, expenses as they happen
2. **Submit** — your entries leave your desk
3. **Manager approves** — one check, with a reason if anything is rejected
4. **Finance confirms** — the second stage of the chain closes
5. **See status anytime** — draft, submitted, pending manager, pending finance, approved or rejected, always visible

## Entry statuses at a glance

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Draft** | Saved on your desk; you can still edit it |
| **Submitted** | Sent for review |
| **Pending manager** | Waiting on the first approval |
| **Pending finance** | Approved by your manager, waiting on finance |
| **Approved** | Final — trusted data |
| **Rejected** | Returned with a reason; correct it and resubmit |

## One worked example

An engineer logs 6 hours against a project activity on Tuesday and submits. The manager approves; finance confirms. Done — no spreadsheets, no chasing.

That is the whole loop: record once, submit, and watch the status move until the entry is approved.

## Working with entries

- **One entry per activity per date** — your hours are captured at the source, against the activity where the work happened.
- **You can edit an entry while it is in draft or submitted**, so mistakes are correctable before approval.
- **Approved entries are final.** A rejected entry shows the reason for rejection and can be corrected — a mistake never silently sticks.

## Expenses

- Claim expenses — mileage, meal, accommodation, and more — with an amount and a receipt upload.
- Mileage is logged with the distance in kilometers.
- Expenses route through the same two-stage approval chain as time entries: manager, then finance.
- Your expense status is visible at every step, just like time entries.

## What you can see

Visibility follows the org hierarchy. Employees see their own entries at every status. Managers additionally see the data for the people under them, and finance sees what the approval chain routes to them — your role determines which approval step you perform. The full role picture is on the [Adopters](Adopters.md) page.

## If something goes wrong

- **Rejected?** Read the reason, fix it, and resubmit.
- **Wondering what a status means?** See the table above, or check the [FAQ](FAQ.md).
- **New to Hourglass?** Your admin invites you via a code or link — see [Getting started](Getting-Started.md).
