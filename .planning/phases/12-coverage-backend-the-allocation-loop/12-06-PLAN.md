    ToCoverQueue(ctx, orgID): single query — SELECT te.id, te.user_id, COALESCE(u.name,''), te.entry_date, te.activity_id, COALESCE(a.name,''), te.hours, COALESCE(SUM(ca.hours),0) FROM time_entries te LEFT JOIN coverage_allocations ca ON ca.entry_id = te.id AND ca.entry_type = 'time' LEFT JOIN users u ON u.id = te.user_id LEFT JOIN activities a ON a.id = te.activity_id WHERE te.org_id = $1 AND te.status = 'approved' AND te.is_deleted = false GROUP BY te.id, u.name, a.name HAVING COALESCE(SUM(ca.hours),0) < te.hours ORDER BY te.entry_date, te.id — rows include covered + uncovered (hours − sum); float compare via cents in Go after scan (Pitfall 6 exact predicate; D-06 rows present because uncovered includes zero-allocation entries).
